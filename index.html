About the Journal

The International Journal of Engineering Innovation, Research and Development (IJEIRD) is an international Open Access and Double-Blind Peer-Reviewed journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research in engineering, technology, and interdisciplinary scientific domains.


The journal provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, engineers, and industry professionals to publish innovative findings, experimental investigations, and technological advancements.

Open Access

All published articles are freely accessible worldwide.

Peer Review

Rigorous Double-Blind Peer Review process.

Biannual Publication

Two issues are published annually.
