About the Journal

The International Journal of Engineering Innovation, Research and Development (IJEIRD) is an international Open Access and Double-Blind Peer-Reviewed journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research in engineering, technology, and interdisciplinary scientific studies.

The journal provides a global platform for researchers, academicians, scientists, scholars, and industry professionals to share innovative ideas, advanced technologies, experimental findings, and research developments that contribute to scientific and industrial progress.

IJEIRD publishes original research papers, review articles, technical reports, case studies, and scholarly communications from various branches of engineering and applied sciences.

The journal encourages interdisciplinary collaboration and technological advancement that addresses industrial, environmental, and societal challenges through innovative research and scientific excellence.

Aim and Scope

The journal aims to provide a high-quality international platform for researchers, engineers, academicians, and industry professionals to publish innovative and original research in engineering, technology, and interdisciplinary sciences.

IJEIRD encourages the dissemination of emerging research findings, novel methodologies, analytical models, and engineering solutions that support scientific and industrial advancement.

Biannual Publication

Two issues are published annually.

Peer Review

Rigorous Double-Blind Peer Review process.

Open Access

All published articles are freely accessible worldwide.

IJEIRD welcomes

Original research, review, technical notes, case studies, short communications, and interdisciplinary studies from various domains of engineering and applied sciences.
