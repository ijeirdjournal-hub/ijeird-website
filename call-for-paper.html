Call for Papers

The International Journal of Engineering Innovation, Research and Development (IJEIRD) invites researchers, academicians, scientists, industry professionals, and scholars to submit original and high-quality research papers for publication in upcoming issues of the journal.

IJEIRD is an Open Access, Double-Blind Peer-Reviewed international journal dedicated to publishing innovative research and advanced developments in the fields of engineering, technology, and interdisciplinary studies.

The journal provides a global platform for researchers to share scientific knowledge, emerging technologies, practical applications, and innovative solutions that contribute to academic and industrial advancement.

Types of Papers Accepted

  • Original Research Articles
  • Review Papers
  • Case Studies
  • Technical Reports
  • Short Communications
  • Innovative Project-Based Studies

Submission Guidelines

Authors are requested to submit original manuscripts that have not been published or submitted elsewhere for publication.

The manuscript should follow the journal formatting guidelines and include proper references, figures, tables, and author details. Papers must be written in clear and concise English.

Publication Frequency

IJEIRD is a Biannual journal and publishes two issues per year. The journal is committed to maintaining a timely publication schedule while ensuring high standards of peer review and academic quality.

Each issue features original research articles, review papers, and innovative studies from various engineering and interdisciplinary domains contributed by researchers and professionals from around the world.

Invitation to Researchers

IJEIRD encourages innovative and impactful research contributions that address modern engineering challenges and technological developments.

We welcome submissions from universities, research institutions, industries, and independent researchers from across the globe.

Researchers interested in publishing their work are invited to submit their manuscripts through the journal submission system or contact the editorial office for further details regarding publication and review procedures.
