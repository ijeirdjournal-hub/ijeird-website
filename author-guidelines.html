Author Guidelines

The International Journal of Engineering Innovation, Research and Development (IJEIRD) welcomes the submission of original, high-quality research papers, review articles, case studies, and technical reports related to engineering, technology, and interdisciplinary research domains.

Important Note

Authors are requested to carefully follow the journal guidelines before submitting manuscripts.

1. Manuscript Preparation

  • Title of the Paper
  • Author Name(s) and Affiliation(s)
  • Abstract
  • Keywords
  • Introduction
  • Literature Review (if applicable)
  • Methodology / Experimental Work
  • Results and Discussion
  • Conclusion
  • Future Scope (Optional)
  • Acknowledgement (Optional)
  • References

2. Paper Formatting

3. Abstract and Keywords

The abstract should summarize objectives, methodology, results and conclusions in 150–250 words. Authors should provide 4–6 keywords.

4. References

Authors may follow IEEE, APA, or any standard referencing style consistently throughout the manuscript.

5. Originality and Plagiarism Policy

Submitted manuscripts must be original and free from plagiarism. Any unethical practices may result in rejection.

6. Peer Review Process

  • Originality of Research
  • Technical Quality
  • Research Significance
  • Clarity and Presentation
  • Relevance to Journal Scope

7. Copyright and Publication Ethics

  • Manuscript must be original work.
  • All authors must approve final submission.
  • Proper acknowledgements must be included.
  • No copyright violations.

8. Submission Procedure

Authors can submit manuscripts through the journal submission system or official email.
