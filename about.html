About IJEIRD

The International Journal of Engineering Innovation, Research and Development (IJEIRD) is an international Open Access and Double-Blind Peer-Reviewed scholarly journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research articles, review papers, technical reports, and interdisciplinary studies in engineering, technology, and scientific innovation.

The journal aims to provide a global platform for academicians, researchers, scientists, engineers, industry professionals, and scholars to exchange innovative ideas, recent advancements, practical developments, and research findings across multiple engineering and technological disciplines.

IJEIRD encourages original research contributions in various areas including Computer Science Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Information Technology, Renewable Energy, Robotics, Environmental Engineering, Data Science, and other emerging interdisciplinary domains.

The journal follows a rigorous Double-Blind Peer-Review process to ensure originality, research quality, academic integrity, and scientific significance. All accepted articles are published under an Open Access model to provide free and unrestricted access to scholarly knowledge worldwide.

Open Access

All published articles are freely accessible worldwide without subscription charges.

Peer Review

Every manuscript undergoes a rigorous Double-Blind Peer-Review process.

Publication Frequency

The journal publishes two issues annually under the Biannual publication model.
